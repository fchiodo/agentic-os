Recommended Use Cases

● Dynamic hero selection: Choose the most relevant lead or secondary

story for each customer using browsing and purchase history.

● Audience targeting: Analyze content and customer attributes to select
an audience at an individual level.

● Live inventory: Show recently viewed products while suppressing
out-of-stock items.

● Loyalty personalization: Include rewards, points balance, and birthday offers.

● Category affinity: Tailor campaigns to the categories a customer prefers.

● Regional context: Adjust content for weather, location, and nearby stores.

● Continuous testing: Test subject lines and creative variants while content is active.

● Signal enhancement: Add existing affinity scores as supplemental signals.

● Promotion optimization: Shift exposure toward stronger-performing creative.

● Broader content support: Keep community and educational stories available.

● Modular assembly: Build messages from approved reusable modules.

● Planning insights: Measure which assets and offers drive engagement.

● Send time and frequency: Identify the optimal cadence for each customer.

● Deployment scale: Launch relevant sends without adding the same production work.
